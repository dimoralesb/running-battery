# VighLarsen2018: Acceleration and deceleration profiles by position, Danish Superliga
Citation: Vigh-Larsen JF, Dalgas U, Andersen TB. Position-specific acceleration and deceleration profiles in elite youth and senior soccer players. J Strength Cond Res, published ahead of print (2017–2018; final volume/pages not checked). doi:10.1519/JSC.0000000000001918. **Our copy is the accepted manuscript.**
Type: match study (single club)
Context: one Danish Superliga club, spring 2015, 8 home matches, 14 senior players, 54 full-match observations; also U19 (4 matches) and U17 (5). **ZXY local positioning system (radio, waist belt), 20 Hz.** The team won 7 of 8 (21–6 goals).
Evidence strength: low. One club, home wins only, small n, pre-2020.

## Definitions and thresholds
- HIR 19.8–25.2 km/h (5.5–7.0 m/s); sprint ≥ 25.2 km/h.
- Acceleration: starts at 1 m/s², counts if it exceeds **2 m/s² for ≥ 0.5 s**, ends below 1 m/s² (same for decelerations).

## Key numbers   (each with page/table/figure)
- Senior full match (Abstract; Results): TD 10,776 ± 107 m; HIR 668 ± 28 m; sprint 143 ± 10 m (mean ± SEM); **accelerations 81 ± 2, decelerations 84 ± 3**.
- By position, full-match accelerations (Table 1, PDF): attackers 80, CM 81, WM 89, WD 91, CD 68; wide players 90 vs central 76.
- **Half-to-half decline (Abstract):** acc + dec −11 ± 3%; HIR −6 ± 4%; TD −5 ± 1%; sprint ns. Accelerations 41 → 37, decelerations 43 → 38 (Results).
- Central players declined in every variable; wide players did not (Fig. 1).
- Over 15-min periods: acc + dec and TD fell across each half; HIR and sprint showed no period differences (Fig. 2).
- U19 had more acc/dec and TD per minute than seniors (Fig. 3).

None.

## Limitations
- One club, all home games, mostly big wins (could reduce late-game running).
- LPS (ZXY) counts not comparable with optical or GPS counts.
- Table 1 is scrambled in the .md and in the PDF layout text; only partial position values recovered.

## Use in Second Wind
- Simulator: relative target: **acc/dec per half −10% or so, larger than HIR (−6%) and TD (−5%), sprint ~flat.**
- Fatigue estimator: acceleration counts may be a more sensitive within-match fatigue signal than HSR, but evidence is thin and the counts are system-dependent.
- Agents / claims: "accelerations drop more than running distance in the second half" → mixed (this study and Akenhead2013 yes; others no, per the paper's discussion).
- Overlay / UI: none.

## Ideas or questions it raised
- Compare with Akenhead2013 (also acc decline) when setting the simulator's second-half acceleration drop.
