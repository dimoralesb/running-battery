# Morgans2025c: Running and accelerations by match outcome, location and opponent (one English club, five seasons)
Citation: Morgans R, Radnor J, Fonseca J, Rhodes D, Ryan B, et al. Comparison of running and accelerometry variables based on match outcome, match location and quality of opponent in elite professional soccer players. A five-season study. Biol Sport 2025;42(1):67–79. doi:10.5114/biolsport.2024.136092
Type: match study (single club, longitudinal)
Context: one English club, 2018/19–2022/23: three Championship seasons then two EPL seasons (promoted after 2020/21); 46 outfield players; mostly 4-3-3. **GPS STATSports Apex 18 Hz** (exported at 10 Hz). Players with ≥ 60 min.
Evidence strength: low. One club, mixed divisions, GPS.

## Definitions and thresholds
- Opponent quality: top six / mid-table / bottom six by previous season ranking.
- Running zones and acceleration thresholds as in the paper's Methods (dwell time noted; tables garbled in .md).

## Key numbers   (each with page/table/figure)
- **Team level: no differences in any variable by match outcome** (Table 1B; Results).
- vs top-six opponents: more m/min and decelerations (d = 0.21–0.32) (Table 1C).
- Home vs away: only accelerations differ (more at home, d = 0.145).
- Position-level effects exist (e.g. CMs more TD in wins than losses, d = 0.34; CBs more HSR in losses) but are many small comparisons.
