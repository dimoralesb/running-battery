# Chen2025: Reference values for 13 positional roles at UEFA Euro 2024
Citation: Chen S, Zmijewski P, Bradley PS. Establishing reference values for the match running performances of thirteen specific positional roles at UEFA Euro 2024. Biol Sport 2025;42(3):257–268. doi:10.5114/biolsport.2025.148535 (open access, PMC12244375)
Type: match study (tournament)
Context: all 51 Euro 2024 matches, 24 national teams, optical tracking (Hawk-Eye); 544 full-match observations (90 min + added time, extra time excluded), 13 roles (e.g. CB in back four n = 122, FB n = 92, WB n = 31, DM n = 59, CM n = 38, AM n = 45, WF n = 18, lone FW n = 21, paired FW n = 14, WM n = 6). Raw data re-filtered by the authors.
Evidence strength: medium. Elite and recent, but tournament (not league), small n for some roles, most numbers only in figures.

## Definitions and thresholds
- High-intensity running (HIR) ≥ 20 km/h (5.56 m/s), sprinting ≥ 25 km/h (6.94 m/s). Close to, but not the same as, the EPL 5.5 / 7.0 m/s.
- Peak 1-min HIR: rolling average.

## Key numbers   (each with page/table/figure)
- **Peak 1-min HIR (Results, "Match-play periods"):** FB 69.0 ± 24.0 m, WB 74.4 ± 22.0 m, CB (back four) 50.8 ± 17.4 m; CB3C 54.1 ± 16.1, CB3O 58.1 ± 22.4, DM 61.3 ± 22.7, single pivot 52.8 ± 20.5, CM 62.4 ± 15.1, AM 63.2 ± 14.7, WM 61.0 ± 16.7, WF 57.0 ± 13.4, lone FW 54.8 ± 13.0, paired FW 58.5 ± 22.9 m.
- **Halves (same section):** TD per minute fell in the 2nd half for most roles (ES 0.5–1.3; not DM1, WB, WM); **HIR/min small declines (ES 0.1–0.4, mostly ns)**; sprint/min maintained (FB and WB slightly up, ns).
- Variation within a role (Discussion): CV TD ~5–10%, HIR ~20–40%, sprint ~30–70%.
- Ranges quoted in Discussion: CM/DM TD ~11.4–11.9 km, CB ~9.7–10.1 km; CB/DM HIR ~500–800 m, sprint ~130–140 m. Full role means only in Fig. 1A–D (not extracted).
- Top-ten sprint speeds 34.8–35.7 km/h (9.7–9.9 m/s), mostly FB/WB and forwards (Fig. 1D).
- Pass completion by role is in Table 1 (garbled in .md; not extracted).

## Limitations
- Tournament, national teams, few matches per player (3 ± 1).
- Distances lower than World Cup 2022 with the same thresholds; authors blame system/filtering differences.
- Not segmented by effective playing time.

## Use in Second Wind
- Simulator: **peak 1-min HIR target by role** (~50–75 m; wide defenders highest, CBs lowest). Agrees with Ju2022's 50–85 m. **Second-half decline target: TD/min clearly down; HIR/min slightly down; sprint/min flat.**
- Fatigue estimator: the flat sprint/min between halves reinforces "fatigue shows in volume and repeated efforts more than top-end sprinting".
- Agents / claims: role labels matter (WB vs FB, CB in 3 vs 4). Large within-role variation means "below typical for his role" needs wide bands.
- Overlay / UI: role-specific peak-minute gauge.
