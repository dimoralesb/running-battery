# Rostami2025: Critical velocity from 400 m and 1600 m runs in young Iranian players
Citation: Rostami M, Alizadeh R, Rezaeinezhad N (2025). Prediction of endurance performance and repeated sprint ability based on critical velocity in soccer players depending on their playing position. Health Nexus 3(1):55–63. doi:10.61838/kman.hn.3.1.7
Type: cross-sectional, regression.
Context: 60 amateur players of one team in Ilam, Iran, 16–20 y. All-out 400 m and 1600 m runs (hand-timed, on separate days); RAST (6 × 35 m). CV and anaerobic distance capacity (ADC) from the two runs.
Evidence strength: **very low; not used for numbers.** Health Nexus is a young journal from KMAN Publication (Crossref checked 2026-10-17); several basic errors below.

## Key numbers (as printed, units corrected by us)
- 400 m 67.9 ± 4.9 s; 1600 m 436 ± 41 s (Table 3). **CV 11.86 ± 1.35 km/h (3.29 ± 0.38 m/s)**, ADC 0.17 ± 0.03 km (≈ 175 m) (Table 4). Check: (1600 − 400)/(436.4 − 67.9) = 3.26 m/s; ADC = 400 − 3.26 × 67.9 ≈ 179 m (derived).

## Limitations and errors
- Units mislabelled: the "m/s" speeds in Table 3 are km/h (21.3 "m/s" for a 68 s 400 m); CV is in km/h and ADC in km without saying so.
- Height and weight are swapped in Table 1 (weight 175 "kg", height 69 "cm").
- "All three CV models give identical values" (Table 4) is automatic with only two runs; the authors work this out as if it were a finding.
- Regressing 400 m and 1600 m times on a CV and ADC computed from those same times is circular (Table 7).
- No correction for many tests on 60 players split into six positions.

## Use in Running Battery
None for calibration. It only adds a two-run ADC (~175 m) to the pile showing that footballers' D′ mostly comes out in the 100–210 m range, with the value depending on the test.
