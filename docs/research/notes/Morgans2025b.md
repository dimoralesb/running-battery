# Morgans2025b: Accelerations and decelerations by position, Premier League vs Ligue 1
Citation: Morgans R, Ju W, Radnor J, et al. The positional demands of explosive actions in elite soccer: comparison of English Premier League and French Ligue 1. Biol Sport 2025;42(1):81–87. doi:10.5114/biolsport.2025.139083
Type: match study (two clubs)
Context: one EPL club and one Ligue 1 club, 2020/21–2021/22, 144 matches (76 EPL, 68 L1), 58 outfield players, full matches (≥ 90 min) only. **GPS, STATSports Apex 18 Hz** (not optical). EPL team: 4-3-3 or 3-5-2.
Evidence strength: low-medium. One club per league, small positional n (EPL FB n = 3, AM n = 4, CF n = 4), GPS-derived counts.

## Definitions and thresholds
- Acceleration > +3 m/s², deceleration < −3 m/s², minimum duration 0.5 s; reported per minute of playing time (Methods).

## Key numbers   (each with page/table/figure)
- **EPL accelerations per min (Table 1):** CB 0.81 ± 0.15, FB 0.96 ± 0.19, CM 0.87 ± 0.19, AM 0.97 ± 0.29, CF 0.92 ± 0.12. **Decelerations per min:** CB 0.86 ± 0.15, FB 1.17 ± 0.13, CM 1.14 ± 0.19, AM 1.04 ± 0.30, CF 0.91 ± 0.12.
- L1 lower in every position (e.g. CB 0.60 acc/min). League effect: EPL > L1, d = 0.72 (acc), 0.65 (dec) (Results).
- Position main effect small (η² 0.034 acc, 0.076 dec): FB and AM > CB and CM; CB lowest decelerations.
- Introduction cites ~90–120 accelerations vs ~15–30 sprints per match (p.81; secondary).

## Derived (ours)
- 0.8–1.0 acc/min × ~95 min ≈ **75–95 accelerations > 3 m/s² per match (GPS)**. Castellano2025 (broadcast tracking, ≥0.7 s) gives only ~6–9 per match above 3 m/s². A ~10-fold difference from system, filtering and dwell rules.

## Limitations

## Use in Running Battery
- Simulator: shape only (FB/AM highest, CB lowest, decelerations ≥ accelerations). **Do not use absolute counts as targets.**
- Fatigue estimator: acceleration counts depend so much on the system and filter that we should only track **relative** change within a match (e.g. first vs second half), never compare to literature counts.
- Agents / claims: "Premier League players accelerate more than Ligue 1 players" is weakly supported (one club each).
- Overlay / UI: none.

## Ideas or questions it raised
