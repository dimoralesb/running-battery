# Allen2024: Evolution of running load in the Premier League, 2014/15 to 2018/19
Citation: Allen T, Taberner M, Zhilkin M, Rhodes D. Running more than before? The evolution of running load demands in the English Premier League. Int J Sports Sci Coach 2024;19(2):779–787 (online 2023). doi:10.1177/17479541231164507. **Published Sage version** (9 PDF pages = journal pp.779–787). Page numbers below are journal pages. (This note replaces an earlier one written from the accepted manuscript, which had no tables.)
Type: match study (league-wide, longitudinal, team and position level)
Context: English Premier League, all clubs, seasons 2014/15 to 2018/19. 1,397 players; 1,872 games collated; 27 without tracking and 238 not finishing 11 v 11 removed, leaving **1,634 games** (p.780). Optical tracking: **TRACAB Gen-4**, 6 semi-automated HD cameras, **25 Hz**, 36 m mounting height; data are TRACAB's physical summary files from the EPL portal (p.781). Positions from Opta: GK, CB, FB, DM, CM, AM, WM, CF (p.780). 2019/20 excluded because of the switch to Second Spectrum and COVID (p.780).
Evidence strength: **high.** Whole league, 5 seasons, one system, per position with SDs. Two caveats: it is TRACAB, not today's Second Spectrum, and the data end in 2018/19.

## Definitions and thresholds
| Metric | Definition (p.781) |
|---|---|
| TD | total distance over the full game, incl. stoppages and added time |
| HID | > 5.5 m/s (19.8 km/h), **includes** sprint (HSR 5.5–7 m/s + sprint) |
| SprD | > 7.0 m/s (25.2 km/h) |
| Dwell time | 0.5 s for HID efforts, 1 s for sprint efforts (manufacturer guideline) |
| Accelerations | not analysed: "current optical tracking technologies are not capable of accurately determining acceleration and deceleration efforts" (p.785) |
| Effect size | Cohen's d: trivial < 0.2, small 0.2–0.6, moderate 0.6–1.2, large 1.2–2.0 (p.781) |

**Unit of the position values:** the paper does not say whether they are full-match players only or per appearance. The sum check below (11 slots ≈ team total within 1–4%) shows they behave like **per-position-slot values per match**.

2014/15 → 2018/19: TD +1% (ES 0.21), HID +12% (ES 0.88), SprD +15% (ES 0.67) (Table 4, p.782).

**Sum check (derived):** 4-2-3-1 (GK, 2 CB, 2 FB, DM, CM, AM, 2 WM, CF) with 2018/19 values gives TD 109,234 m (−0.9% vs team), HID 9,134 m (+0.7%), SprD 2,077 m (+3.8%). The position table is internally consistent with the team totals.

**Same season, two papers (derived):** for 2018/19, Allen2024 (raw TRACAB) gives team HID 9,074 / SprD 2,000; Allen2025 (TRACAB converted to Second Spectrum) gives 8,024 / 1,788 (−12% / −11%). That matches Allen2025's calibration equations (HSR −587 + 0.99×, SprD −85 + 0.92× → HID ≈ 8,170, SprD ≈ 1,755). TD does not match the TD equation: Allen2025 reports 113,003 vs about 108,650 expected. **Unexplained; it doesn't matter for us because we scale to Allen2025 totals directly.**

**Older trends cited, not Allen's data** (p.780–781): Barnes et al. 2014 (Prozone 10 Hz): 2006/07 → 2012/13 HID +30%, SprD +35%. Bush et al. 2015: FB grew most (HID +35%, SprD +62%), attackers least. Nassis et al. predicted +40% HID by 2030; Allen thinks this is an overestimate (p.781).

**System comparability** (p.782): sprint distance can differ by **~50% between tracking systems** (citing refs 7, 13); 25 Hz TRACAB is more accurate than 10 Hz Prozone.

## Limitations
- TRACAB only; today's EPL uses Second Spectrum, which reads high-speed distances about 10–12% lower [Allen2025, Fig. 1].
- Data end in 2018/19. Team HID has risen about 20% and sprint about 30% since (2S scale) [Allen2025, Fig. 2].
- Unit of the position values (full-match only or per appearance) not stated.
- Only matches finishing 11 v 11.
- No accelerations, no counts, no in/out of possession, no time course within the match.
- Positions from Opta labels; 307 players played ≥ 3 positions (Table 3, p.780).
- **Text vs table:** the results text (p.781) says CB, FB, CM, AM and CF had small SprD increases, but Table 4 shows CM (p = 0.1) and AM (p = 0.065) as **not significant**. The abstract and Table 4 agree; the results sentence is wrong.

## Use in Running Battery
- Simulator: **the position shape for calibration.** Take the 2018/19 values (all 8 positions, with SDs for the spread between players) and scale each metric so a full XI adds up to the 2024/25 team totals in Allen2025 (TD ×1.033, HID ×1.059, SprD ×1.163). See synthesis §4.2. The GK row (about 4.6–4.7 km, almost no HID) fills the gap noted with Allen2025.
- Fatigue estimator: none directly. The 5.5 / 7.0 m/s thresholds with 0.5 s / 1 s dwell are the EPL convention; use them.
- Agents / claims: position norms are now sourced: "for a centre-back, about 120–140 m of sprinting is typical". Use them as ranges, with the season they come from. Keep the attribution rule: the +30% HID 2006–13 is Barnes 2014 (cited), not Allen.
- Overlay / UI: "vs typical [position]" bars (TD, HID, sprint) with ±1 SD bands from this table, scaled to 2024/25.

## Ideas or questions it raised
- Position-specific trends differ (CB and CF sprint +23–25%, CM and AM unchanged). So uniform scaling to 2024/25 is a simplification; flag it.
- The SDs (e.g. CB sprint 121 ± 63 m) are large. Between-player spread in the simulator should be wide, not tight around the mean.
- Allen et al. 2026 (Biol Sport, "Possession in motion") would add the in/out-of-possession split; not read.
