# Cihan2022: Critical velocity, anaerobic speed reserve and match running in U17 players
Citation: Cihan H (2022). An analysis of the relationship between critical velocity and anaerobic speed reserve with match running profile in football. Pedagogy Phys Cult Sports 26(6):353–363. doi:10.15561/26649837.2022.0601
Type: cross-sectional correlation, one match.
Context: 15 U17 players (16.6 ± 0.5 y) of the youth team of a Turkish Super League club. Yo-Yo IR1 (maximal aerobic speed = final speed), 30 m sprint with photocells (top speed from 10–30 m), all-out 800 m and 2400 m runs on grass (hand-timed) for CV and ADC; one 11 v 11 match with 10 Hz GPS (GPSports EVO).
Evidence strength: **low; the match data are unusable.** Small regional journal (use with care), single author, n = 15, one match. Crossref checked 2026-10-17.

## Key numbers (Table 2, p.356)
- **CV 14.43 ± 0.93 km/h (4.01 ± 0.26 m/s)**, range 13.6–17.0 km/h. Check: (2400 − 800)/(572.9 − 172.4) = 4.00 m/s (derived).
- ADC 116 ± 38 m (46–185).
- Yo-Yo IR1 final speed 16.7 ± 0.7 km/h (4.65 m/s); VO₂max estimated 53.4 ± 3.6 ml/kg/min. CV / Yo-Yo final speed = 0.86 (derived). The Yo-Yo IR1 final speed is an intermittent-test speed, not the speed at VO₂max.
- Top speed over 10–30 m: 28.1 ± 0.8 km/h (7.8 m/s).

## Limitations and errors (read critically)
- **The match data cover about 16 minutes, not 90.** Total distance 2,288 ± 482 m, which is what the five speed bands add up to (2,286 m), while the mean speed of 8.34 km/h over 90 min would give about 12.5 km. The times in the heart-rate zones add up to about 985 s (16 min). Maximum match speed 19.5 km/h vs a tested top speed of 28 km/h, and 2.5 m above 21 km/h, are also implausible for a full match.
- So every match correlation and regression (Tables 3–4, R² up to 0.83 on n = 15 with three predictors) is unusable.

## Use in Second Wind
- **CS prior**: one more test-based value, from trained U17 academy players: CV 4.0 ± 0.26 m/s by a two-run time trial. That is close to our professional prior (4.1 ± 0.3), and its SD matches ours. Academy youth, not professionals, so supporting only.
- **D′**: the two-run ADC (116 m) is lower than 3MT values (173–288 m); D′ depends on the test.
- **Claims**: none (the match part is broken).
