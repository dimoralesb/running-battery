# Lord2020: Maximal mean and "critical" speed from match GPS, A-League vs sub-elite
Citation: Lord C, Blazevich AJ, Abbiss CR, Drinkwater EJ, Ma'ayah F (2020). Comparing maximal mean and critical speed and metabolic powers in elite and sub-elite soccer. Int J Sports Med 41(4):219–226. doi:10.1055/a-1065-2100
Type: observational, two teams over one season each.
Context: one A-League team (n = 24, 432 match files) and one sub-elite youth NPL Western Australia team (n = 22, 19.1 y, 416 files). GPSports SPI HPU, positions at 5 Hz interpolated to 15 Hz. Rolling mean speed over 1, 5, 10, 60, 300 and 600 s; the best value per match ("maximal mean speed", MMS). "CS" from a linear fit RS = S′·(1/t) + CS over those six windows.
Evidence strength: **medium for the match distances, low for the "CS".** Established journal; Crossref checked 2026-10-17.
**The .md is the title page only; read from the PDF.**

## Key numbers
- A-League by position, per match (Table 1): total distance 10,356 (CB) – 11,838 m (CM); > 5.5 m/s 520 (CB) – 798 m (ST); > 7 m/s 71 (CM) – 181 m (FB).
- "CS", A-League (Table 2): CB 3.6 ± 0.3, FB 3.9 ± 0.2, CM 3.8 ± 0.2, WM 4.0 ± 0.3, ST 3.9 ± 0.2 m/s. Sub-elite youth: 3.7–3.9 m/s. Only centre-backs differed between levels.
- MMS, A-League (Table 2): 1 s 7.8–8.3 m/s; 60 s 3.0–3.5; **300 s 2.3–2.6; 600 s 2.1–2.4 m/s**.

## Equations
RS = S′/t + CS (speed); d = CS·t + D′ for the field tests (Methods, Data analysis).

## Main message
Elite players reach higher peak speeds and metabolic powers over 1–5 s; positions differ; "CS" and critical metabolic power barely separate the levels.

## Limitations and errors (read critically)
- **The "CS" is a fitting artefact, not a sustainable speed.** In the same table, the best 5- and 10-minute means (2.1–2.6 m/s) are well **below** the "CS" (3.6–4.0). A fit of RS = S′/t + CS to the Table 2 means reproduces the published CS to 0.01–0.03 m/s (CB 3.58, FB 3.85, CM 3.81, WM 3.97, ST 3.87; our check, 2026-10-17), with S′ ≈ 5 m. The 1/t term is dominated by the 1–10 s windows, so the intercept lands between sprint and cruising speeds. S′ ≈ 5 m is nothing like a D′ (≈ 180 m).
- The validation it cites (Lord 2019, J Hum Kinet, youth players) is not in our set.
- One team per level; results per position from 4–6 players.

## Use in Second Wind
- **Estimator (synthesis 3.2.5, option A)**: one more reason not to fit CS from match data. Depending on the model, the same kind of data gives 1.7 m/s [Roecker2017], 2.0 m/s [Mizelman2024] or 3.6–4.0 m/s (this paper). The number is a property of the fit.
- **Agents / claims**: block any per-player "match critical speed" (claim 29).
- **Simulator**: the A-League position distances are a loose plausibility check only; EPL targets come from Allen2024/2025.
- Pimenta2025 cites this group's method (its ref. 30) as having "validated" match-derived CS of 13.7–14.4 km/h; given the artefact above, we don't use that.
