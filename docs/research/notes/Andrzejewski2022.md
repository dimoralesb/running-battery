# Andrzejewski2022: Team success, technical and running performance in the Bundesliga
Citation: Andrzejewski M, Oliva-Lozano JM, Chmura P, Chmura J, Czarniecki S, Kowalczuk E, Rokita A, Muyor JM, Konefał M. Analysis of team success based on match technical and running performance in a professional soccer league. BMC Sports Sci Med Rehabil 2022;14:82. doi:10.1186/s13102-022-00473-7
Type: match study (league-wide, team level)
Context: Bundesliga 2017/18 and 2018/19, 612 matches, 1,224 team observations. IMPIRE/VIS.TRACK optical, 25 Hz. Sprint > 22.68 km/h (6.3 m/s).
Evidence strength: medium (whole league, two seasons; correlations on 36 team-seasons).

## Definitions and thresholds
- Teams grouped high (1–6), medium (7–15), low (16–18) by final ranking. Running split **with and without ball possession, as totals** (not per minute).

## Key numbers   (each with page/table/figure)
- Strongest correlations with season points are technical: goals scored r = 0.90, assists 0.88, shots on goal allowed −0.87, goals conceded −0.81, possession 0.78, successful open-play passes 0.78 (Results; Table 3).
- Running (Table 3): **TD with possession r = 0.75; TD without possession r = −0.70**; sprint actions with possession 0.55.
- Sprint distance with possession (Table 2): high-ranked 2,168 ± 164 m vs medium 1,925 and low 1,869 m per match (team); without possession no difference (~2,107–2,141 m).

## Equations
None.

## Limitations
- Totals by phase mostly reflect **how long** a team has the ball; this is why "TD without possession" correlates negatively here but out-of-possession running **per minute** correlates positively in Allen2026b. Not a real contradiction.
- Bundesliga, older seasons.

## Use in Running Battery
- Simulator: none.
- Fatigue estimator: none.
- Agents / claims: supports "technical/possession variables explain success much more than running". Teach the Verifier: compare phases **per minute of that phase**, never as totals.
- Overlay / UI: none.
