# Carpels2026: Academy, reserve and first-team physical profiles in one Scottish Premiership club
Citation: Carpels T, Kemi OJ (2026). Physical profiling in elite professional men's football: academy, reserve and first team variations in fitness and performance. Open Access J Sports Med 17(1):2697533. doi:10.1080/11791543.2026.2697533
Type: cross-sectional comparison within one club, one season (2018–19), with prospective training and match data.
Context: Scottish Premiership club also playing the Europa League; first team (FT) n = 25, reserves n = 15, under-18 n = 23. Fitness at the end of pre-season: 30-15 Intermittent Fitness Test (IFT); VO₂max **estimated** from the final IFT speed with Buchheit's 2008 equation, not measured. Training and matches with Catapult OptimEye X4 (10 Hz GPS). Matches: mid-October to mid-December, all teams on 2 matches a week.
Evidence strength: **low–medium.** Small open-access journal (Taylor & Francis), single club and season, small subgroups. Crossref checked 2026-10-17 (published 2026-07-04).

## Key numbers
- **First team: VO₂max 56.3 ± 3.6 ml/kg/min** (estimated), final IFT speed 20.6 ± 1.5 km/h (5.72 ± 0.42 m/s), age 26.0 ± 5.2 y, 79.0 ± 5.9 kg (Table 1). Coefficient of variation 6% for VO₂max and 7% for IFT speed (derived).
- Academy players: VO₂max ~7% lower (10% after scaling by body mass^0.75); IFT speed not different between teams (Results, Player characteristics).
- **First-team matches** (73 player-matches, Table 3): total distance 10,195 ± 1,105 m; 5.5–7 m/s (19.8–25.2 km/h) 501 ± 156 m; > 7 m/s (25.2 km/h) 149 ± 88 m; peak speed 8.51 ± 0.4 m/s; 106 ± 12 m/min.
- Academy players ran **more** in matches than the first team: +539 m (U18) and +567 m (reserves) total, +18–20% high-speed distance (Results; Table 3), **despite lower aerobic fitness**.
- Oldest reserves (≥ 18 y) vs youngest first-team players (≤ 23 y): 11,287 vs 10,248 m (Table 4).

## Main message
In this club the first team was fitter but did not run more, in training or in matches. The authors explain it by context: first-team players manage the game to win "while minimising physical effort", academy players play end to end (Discussion).

## Limitations and errors
- VO₂max is estimated from a field test with 0.5 km/h stages, so the values are partly discrete.
- Players with 60–90 min were **scaled up to 90 min** linearly, ignoring the late-match fade (the authors note it, Limitations).
- "Sixteen matches" are listed as 11 league + 1 cup + 5 Europa League = 17 (Methods, Match).
- The .md and the PDF text layer both garble Tables 1–3 (columns shifted, SDs split across cells). The first-team columns above were read from `pdftotext -layout`.

## Use in Running Battery
- **CS spread (decision 13)**: the cleanest between-player spread of aerobic fitness in a professional first team we have (6% CV), already used to set the CS SD (synthesis 3.2.3). Now read in full; the numbers hold.
- **Agents / claims**: strong support for "how much a player runs does not tell you how fit he is". Less fit academy players out-ran fitter professionals in comparable matches. That blocks "he ran more, so he's fitter" and "he ran less, so he's less fit" (claim 34) and backs our framing of work done rather than capacity (decision 14).
